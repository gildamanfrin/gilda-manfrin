About

Gilda Manfrin is a Milan-based creative working across content direction, strategy and visual communication.

Her practice brings together commissioned projects, editorial research and teaching at NABA and IED, where she works with fashion communication, digital strategy and visual methods.

Alongside her visual work, she composes and records music for piano and electronics.
